**How to use them**
- **The last week:** one sheet a day. Cover the right-hand column of each table (or the second half of each bullet) and say it out loud.
- **The day before:** skim all thirteen (Parts X and XII only if your target role needs them), and reread the chapter section for anything that felt shaky.
- **Interview morning:** only Parts IX and XI, which cover how to run the conversation rather than facts, plus Part XIII's last two sections before a staff or architect loop.
